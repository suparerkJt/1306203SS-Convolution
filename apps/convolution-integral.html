Convolution Integral (LTI CT Systems)

y(t) = ∫ x(τ) · h(t − τ) dτ

Time boundaries

Sets the range of τ and t on all three plots (t1 − t0 ≥ 1). Signals start at t = 0 (a unit step starts at its shift time T0).
How to read the plots
  1. Flip h(τ) to get h(−τ).
  2. Shift it by t to get h(t−τ).
  3. Multiply it with x(τ).
  4. Integrate: the shaded area is y(t).
