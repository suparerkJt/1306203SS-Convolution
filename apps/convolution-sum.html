Convolution Sum (LTI DT Systems)

y[n] = Σ x[k] · h[n − k]

Index boundaries

Sets the range of k and n on all three plots (integers, n1 − n0 between 5 and 120). Signals start at n = 0 (a unit step starts at its shift m).
How to read the plots
  1. Flip h[k] to get h[−k].
  2. Shift it by n to get h[n−k].
  3. Multiply it with x[k], sample by sample.
  4. Sum all the products: the total is y[n].
